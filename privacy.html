Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Code Relay Services ("we", "us") collects through coderelay.in, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you send us a message through the contact form, we receive:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • The service you are interested in
  • The message you write to us

We do not ask for payment details on this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send any information you ask for.
  • To prepare proposals or plans you have requested.
  • To keep reasonable business records of our conversations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. We use trusted third-party services to host this website and deliver messages to us.

Cookies and analytics

At the time of writing, this website does not set cookies of its own and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, we will update this page.

How long we keep it

We keep your message for as long as we need it to respond to you and to keep reasonable business records. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email us at contact@coderelay.in and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to us at contact@coderelay.in.
